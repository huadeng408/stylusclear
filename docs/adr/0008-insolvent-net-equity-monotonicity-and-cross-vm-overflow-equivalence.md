# ADR-0008: Insolvent Net-Equity Monotonicity, GCD Lot-Step Quantization, and Five-Endpoint Overflow Equivalence

- **Status**: Accepted (`2026-11-18`)
- **Context**: Independent four-agent adversarial fuzzing and compiler-compatibility review of `StylusClear v0.3.0` (`commit 3190836`) identified:
  1. **Stage 4 Insolvent Net-Equity Drain (`CRIT-01`)**: An already margin-deficient account (`pre_risk.im_surplus_wad < 0`) paying down high-IM debt releases gross-position margin penalties (`ΔLiab * im_bps`). Trading within the `±20%` price band at an off-market price could increase `im_surplus_wad` and `health_wad` while reducing true un-haircutted `net_equity_wad = gross_collateral_wad - total_liability_wad`, transferring bad debt to a confederate; similarly, `haircut_bps == 10_000` allowed transferring 100%-haircut collateral at zero `im_surplus_wad` change.
  2. **Sub-Gwei Reference Price & Cross-Language Overflow Forks (`HIGH-01`–`HIGH-03`)**: `ref_price == 0` and 256-bit `mulDivCeil` quotient overflow diverged between Rust `U512` (`LimitPriceViolated`) and Solidity/Python/JS (`DivisionByZero` / `MathOverflow`); `0 < ref_price < 10^9` coarsened lot steps to `1 WAD`; `2^255` cyclic obligations, multi-account `I256` conservation sums, and `I256::MIN` negation diverged 2-vs-2 across endpoints.
  3. **Toolchain & Runtime Gate Precision (`F-R01`–`F-W02`, `HIGH-04`–`HIGH-05`)**: `stylus-sdk 0.10.10` private `stylus_sdk::host::Host` import, `TestVM::mock_call` 4-arg `value` signature, `Stylus.toml` `[contract.deployments]`, Foundry `vm.etch` storage isolation across `golden_01..04`, `node --input-type=module` in `demo-sepolia-live.sh`, WASM `reference-types` (`0x25/0x26/0xD0/0xD2`) & float-truncation (`0xA8..0xB1`) scanning, and `archcheck` brace-balanced `#[cfg(test)]` stripping.
- **Decision**:
  1. **Strict Haircut Cap & Net-Equity Monotonicity**: Enforce `haircut_bps < 10_000` (`InvalidHaircut` at `100%`) so all collateral retains positive marginal weight, compute `gross_collateral_wad` and `net_equity_wad` in `AccountRiskMetrics`, and require `post_risk.net_equity_wad >= pre_risk.net_equity_wad` whenever `pre_risk.im_surplus_wad < 0` or `post_risk.im_surplus_wad < 0` across Rust, Solidity, Python, and JS.
  2. **512-Bit Cross-Multiply Band & GCD Exact Step Quantization**: Reject `ref_price == 0` with `InvalidAssetConfig`, evaluate the price protection band via division-free 512-bit cross multiplication (`price_diff * 10_000 > ref_price * max_dev_bps`), and generalize `lot_step_for_price(p) = max(10^9, 10^18 / gcd(p, 10^18))` so sub-Gwei cross-pair prices clear fractional lots with zero rounding overshoot.
  3. **Canonical Address Cycle Invariance & Five-Endpoint Overflow Parity**: Order internal DFS vertex indices by participant `Address` byte order so cycle compression metrics are permutation-invariant; enforce identical checked `U256` `compressed_volume`, checked `I256` conservation accumulators, and `I256::MIN` guards across all 5 endpoints.
  4. **Live Sepolia Domain-Separated Dual Simulation**: Equip `web/engine-ui.mjs` + `web/rpc-client.ts` (`dist/rpc-client.mjs`) to dynamically re-sign built-in deterministic demo intents per target contract (`StylusClearingEngine` vs `SolidityClearingBenchmark`) and active `eth_chainId`, enabling zero-revert Live JSON-RPC side-by-side gas comparison on Arbitrum Sepolia (`421614`) and Orbit L3 (`42161405`).
- **Consequences**: Closes all Critical/High/Medium/Low findings from the 4-subagent final audit while preserving the canonical 19-error ABI (`0xcf36836c`) and `< 24 KB` Stylus WASM footprint.
